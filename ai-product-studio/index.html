Privacy Policy & Support  ·  Last updated: November 23, 2026

Welcome to AI Product Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how Hai Phan ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the AI Product Studio app on iOS. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account

  • Email address, display name, and profile photo (if you sign in with Apple, Google, or email/password)
  • A unique account ID and basic account status (e.g. active, subscription state)

Product Photos & Generated Images

  • Photos you upload of your products, and the images our AI pipeline produces from them (background-removed cutout, AI-generated background, final composited result)
  • These are stored privately in our cloud storage, in a folder scoped to your account only — never in a public bucket

AI Processing

  • When you process a product photo, the image is sent to fal.ai (third-party AI infrastructure) to remove the background and, when you generate a new scene, to generate the new background image
  • Your product's actual pixels are never re-generated or redrawn by AI — only the background is AI-generated; the product itself is locked and composited back in deterministically
  • Scene/style choices you make are turned into a text prompt, which is also sent to the AI provider to generate the background
  • Uploaded images, prompts, and generated outputs automatically pass through moderation checks before being shown to you; content that fails this check is blocked and not shown, and any credit spent on it is refunded in full

Credit & Subscription

  • Your credit balance and a history of how credits were earned/spent
  • Subscription status and plan, verified through Apple's StoreKit — we never see or store your payment card details; Apple handles billing directly

Device & Usage

  • Basic app usage analytics (e.g. which screens are used, whether a generation succeeded) via Firebase Analytics, to understand how the app is used and improve it
  • Device/notification token, if you enable notifications, to let us notify you when a generation job finishes

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account, credit balance, and subscription
  • Process your product photos through the AI pipeline (background removal, background generation, moderation, compositing) and show you the result
  • Save your products, Brand Studio presets, and generation history so you can reuse and manage them
  • Detect and block content that violates our content guidelines before it reaches you
  • Understand app usage and fix problems

3. Storage & Security

Your account data, product photos, and generated images are stored on Google Firebase (Firestore database and Cloud Storage), in a private location scoped to your account only. Access rules enforce that only you can read or write your own data — we do not expose it publicly, and we do not log the full content of your prompts or raw image bytes beyond what's needed to run moderation checks.

6. Device Permissions

Photo Library — lets you pick a product photo to upload. Requested only when you first try to upload a photo, not at app launch.

Camera — used only when you choose to take a new photo of your product directly in the app.

Notifications — optional, used to let you know when an AI generation job finishes.

You can deny or revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, product photos, generated images, Brand Studio presets, generation history, and credit/purchase history. This action cannot be undone.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel an active Apple subscription. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple ID subscription settings to stop future billing.

You can also request a copy of your data from Profile → Export My Data.

8. Children's Privacy

9. Your Rights

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI redraw or distort my actual product?

No. Only the background/scene is AI-generated. Your product's real pixels are locked ("Product Lock") and composited back in deterministically — the product itself is never passed through a generative AI model.

What happens to content that gets blocked by moderation?

If an uploaded photo, prompt, or generated result fails our automated content checks, it's blocked and not shown to you, and any credit you spent is fully refunded.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and all associated data and cannot be undone. It does not cancel an active Apple subscription — cancel that separately in your Apple ID settings.

Why does AI Product Studio ask for Photos or Camera permission?

Photos access lets you pick a product photo to upload; Camera access lets you take a new one directly in the app. Both are requested only at the point you use that feature, and you can deny or revoke either at any time in iOS Settings.
