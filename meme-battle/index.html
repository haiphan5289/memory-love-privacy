Meme Battle

Privacy Policy & Support  ·  Last updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Meme Battle, a physics-aiming 1v1 brawler where you pick a fighter, pull back, aim, and shoot. This Privacy Policy explains how Hai Phan ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Meme Battle on iOS. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account

  • Meme Battle signs you in anonymously by default — no personal information is required to play Practice mode or try the app
  • If you choose to save your progress, you can link your account to Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email and password. We then receive your email address and a user ID from the sign-in provider (Apple's "Hide My Email" relay address works too)
  • We never see or store your Apple, Google, or email account password

Player Profile

  • The display name you choose
  • No profile photo, bio, or other personal details are collected

Match & Game Data

  • Practice and online match results: fighter picked, shots taken, HP, win/loss outcome, and match history
  • Your Elo rank and its place on the global leaderboard
  • Friends you add and battle invitations you send or receive
  • Daily mission progress and daily check-in streaks
  • In-game coin balance and cosmetic items unlocked or equipped from the coin shop — this is an in-game currency earned by playing; Meme Battle does not process real-money payments

Usage & Diagnostics

  • Firebase Analytics: in-app events such as "match started" or "character selected", tied to an app-instance identifier, not to your name, email, or user ID

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and secure your account, and keep your progress if you choose to save it
  • Run matches: matchmaking, rooms and invite codes, the authoritative combat result, and turn timing
  • Show your rank, match history, and the leaderboard
  • Run friends, invitations, daily missions, check-in rewards, and the coin shop
  • Understand how the app is used and improve features

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data. Meme Battle has no advertising SDK.

3. What Other Players Can See

  • Your display name and chosen fighter are visible to your opponent during a match
  • Your display name, Elo rank, and win/loss record appear on the global leaderboard
  • Friends you've added can see your display name and online status
  • Your email address is never shown to other players

4. Storage & Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and a server we operate on Google Cloud Run (asia-southeast1) that resolves every online match authoritatively, so one player's device can't alter the outcome. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

  • Sign in with Apple  Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with other players as described in Section 3, with the service providers in Section 5 to run the app, or if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

7. Device Permissions

Meme Battle does not request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, or location. The app is landscape-only and needs no special device permissions to play.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

If you save your account, you can delete it at any time from Home → Account → Delete account. This immediately removes your profile, saved stats, matchmaking queue entry, in-game currency, mission progress, and your sign-in account from our systems — there is no waiting period and it cannot be undone.

If you never save your account (an anonymous guest), uninstalling the app removes your local progress; no linked personal information exists to delete.

9. Your Rights

You can view and change your display name in the app, and delete your account and all associated data as described above. For any other request about your data, contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Meme Battle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll reflect significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Meme Battle signs you in anonymously so you can play Practice mode and online matches right away. Linking Apple, Google, or email is only needed if you want your rank, coins, and history to survive a reinstall or follow you to a new phone.

Can other players see my email?

No. Only your chosen display name and your public rank/match record are visible to others.

What happens to my coins and cosmetics if I delete my account?

Everything tied to your account — coins, unlocked cosmetics, rank, and history — is deleted permanently along with the account. This can't be undone.

Does Meme Battle have in-app purchases?

Not currently. The coin shop uses only coins earned by playing.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Home → Account → Delete account inside the app.

Found a Bug or Have a Feature Request?

Email us at freelancerios0502@gmail.com with a description of what happened (and a screenshot, if possible) — we read every message.
