Privacy Policy & Support  ·  Last updated: September 15, 2026

Welcome to SnapMind. This Privacy Policy explains how Hai Phan ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SnapMind app on iOS. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account

None. SnapMind does not require sign-up or login — there is no account, email, or password collected at any point.

Screenshot Data

  • Images you import from your Photos library, capture with the in-app camera, share to SnapMind from another app (Share Extension), or that SnapMind detects automatically in your Screenshots album (you confirm each import)
  • Imported images are copied into the app's own local storage on your device — they are never uploaded to a server we operate

AI Analysis

  • When you import a screenshot, the image is sent to OpenAI's Vision API (model family GPT-4.1) to extract text (OCR), generate a title/summary, and classify it into a category (e.g. Receipt, Booking, Task)
  • Analysis only happens for screenshots you explicitly import — nothing is analyzed in the background
  • Images and extracted text are sent to OpenAI solely to process that request; we do not maintain our own server-side copy of what is sent

Device & Usage

  • Anonymous app configuration values (e.g. your monthly free AI-analysis limit) are fetched from Firebase Remote Config — no screenshot content or personal data is sent as part of this
  • A local counter of how many AI analyses / AI Chat messages you've used this month/day, stored securely in iOS Keychain on your device (via flutter_secure_storage) — this counter is never transmitted anywhere

2. How We Use Information

  • Extract text, generate a title/summary, and classify screenshots you import
  • Enforce free-plan usage limits (monthly AI analyses, daily AI Chat messages)
  • Let you search, filter, and browse your own screenshots by category, date, or keyword — entirely on-device
  • Optionally add detected events, flights, or bookings to your device's calendar (Settings toggle "Auto-add to Calendar", on by default — you can turn it off anytime)
  • Show local reminders you opted into, such as the Daily Digest and Weekly Summary — these are generated entirely on-device from your own library, with no data sent anywhere to produce them

3. Storage & Security

SnapMind has no login and no cloud sync — your screenshots, extracted text, and AI results are stored only in local storage on your device. We do not operate a backend database that holds your screenshots or their contents.

Data sent to OpenAI for analysis travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Handling of that data once received is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy (linked below).

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI — GPT-4.1 Vision (screenshot OCR, summarization, and classification)  Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC) — remote app configuration only (e.g. usage limits); no screenshot content is sent  Privacy Policy

SnapMind does not include any advertising or analytics/tracking SDK.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your screenshots and their contents stay on your device unless you explicitly share them (e.g. using the Share button, or Weekly Summary's share card). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Device Permissions

Photos — lets SnapMind import screenshots you pick, and detect new screenshots in your Screenshots album (you confirm each import before it's added).

Camera — used only when you choose "Take Photo" to capture a new image directly into SnapMind.

Calendar — optional (Settings toggle, on by default). When enabled, SnapMind writes detected events, flights, or lodging bookings to a calendar on your device. You can deny or revoke this anytime in iOS Settings or the in-app toggle.

Notifications — used for the opt-in Daily Digest and Weekly Summary local reminders, generated entirely on-device.

You can deny or revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because SnapMind stores everything locally and has no account, your data stays only as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete an individual screenshot (swipe to delete) or remove everything at once via Settings → Clear all screenshots. Deleting a screenshot from SnapMind does not delete it from your device's Photos library. Uninstalling the app removes all of SnapMind's local data.

8. Children's Privacy

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use SnapMind?

No. SnapMind works fully offline with no login required — everything is stored locally on your device.

How does the AI analyze my screenshots?

When you import a screenshot, SnapMind sends the image to OpenAI's Vision API to extract text, generate a title/summary, and classify it into a category. Analysis only happens for screenshots you explicitly import.

Is there a limit on how many screenshots I can analyze?

The free plan includes a limited number of AI analyses per month. Once you reach the monthly limit, you can still browse, search, and manage screenshots already imported — new AI analysis resumes the following month.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear all screenshots inside the app to remove everything SnapMind has stored. Deleting a screenshot from SnapMind does not delete it from your device's Photos library.

Why does SnapMind ask for Photos or Calendar permission?

Photos access lets SnapMind detect and import your screenshots. Calendar access (optional, toggle in Settings) lets it automatically add detected events and bookings to your calendar. You can deny or revoke either at any time in iOS Settings.
