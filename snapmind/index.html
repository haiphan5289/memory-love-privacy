Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use SnapMind?

No. SnapMind works fully offline with no login required — everything is stored locally on your device.

How does the AI analyze my screenshots?

When you import a screenshot, SnapMind sends the image to an AI vision service to extract text, generate a title/summary, and classify it into a category. Analysis only happens for screenshots you explicitly import.

Is there a limit on how many screenshots I can analyze?

The free plan includes a limited number of AI analyses per month. Once you reach the monthly limit, you can still browse, search, and manage screenshots already imported — new AI analysis resumes the following month.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear all screenshots inside the app to remove everything SnapMind has stored. Deleting a screenshot from SnapMind does not delete it from your device's Photos library.

Why does SnapMind ask for Photos or Calendar permission?

Photos access lets SnapMind detect and import your screenshots. Calendar access (optional, toggle in Settings) lets it automatically add detected events and bookings to your calendar. You can deny or revoke either at any time in iOS Settings.
